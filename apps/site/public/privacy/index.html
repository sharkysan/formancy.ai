Privacy notice

Updated 4 November 2026

Who is responsible

Daniel Bacher, Untertor 17, 8400 Winterthur, Switzerland, info@formancy.ai.

What the pages ask for

The pages of formancy.ai, its documentation and its playground load everything they use from formancy.ai: no font, script or editor from another site, and no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. Every change to the site is checked in a browser, which fails when a page asks another site for anything (0154).

What they keep in your browser

No cookie. The documentation remembers whether you chose its light or dark theme, in your browser’s local storage under the key starlight-theme, and that value stays in your browser. Nothing else is kept: the same browser check fails when a page keeps anything this notice does not name (0232).

What you type into an example

formancy.ai is a set of files, served as they are: there is no server behind it to receive what you type into an example or into the playground, and the pages send it nowhere else. The playground can prepare a request to an AI model for you to copy. The page sends it to no one; if you paste it into a chat service, that service receives it under your account and its own terms.

Cloudflare, which delivers the pages

The website is delivered by Cloudflare, Inc., USA. When you open a page, Cloudflare processes your IP address, the address of the page, the time, your browser’s details and security signals, to deliver the page and to protect the site from abuse. Cloudflare also asks your browser, by a Network Error Logging header, to report to Cloudflare a request that failed; one that succeeded is not reported. Cloudflare states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, its UK extension and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and relies on standard contractual clauses otherwise; its privacy policy names no period for which it keeps these records. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Read from the live site on 4 November 2026: the pages set no cookie, and hold no Cloudflare analytics, Zaraz or Turnstile script.

E-mail

If you write to info@formancy.ai, Cloudflare Email Routing receives the message and forwards it to the operator’s own mailbox. Your address and what you write are used to answer you, kept while the enquiry and any follow-up need them, and then deleted.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and the EU General Data Protection Regulation where it applies, you can ask what data about you is processed, have it corrected or deleted, and object to its processing: write to info@formancy.ai. You can also complain to a supervisory authority, in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.
